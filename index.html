Publications & Articles

Combating Spurious Correlations in Natural Language Inference via Dataset Cartography

Research Paper

Investigated the reliance of pre-trained language models on spurious correlations and dataset artifacts within Natural Language Inference (NLI) benchmarks. Utilizing the ELECTRA-small architecture, the study employed Dataset Cartography to map training dynamics and identify "easy-to-learn" artifacts. By filtering these artifacts and retraining on harder data subsets, the mitigated model achieved a 47% absolute improvement in accuracy on challenging validation slices, significantly outperforming standard baselines on robust reasoning tasks.

Enhancing Fraud Detection in Insurance Claims Using Advanced Machine Learning Techniques

Research Paper

Authored a research paper on automating insurance fraud detection using the Oracle automobile fraudulent claim dataset. The study implemented and compared Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) and Deep Residual neural networks, utilizing SMOTE data augmentation and standardization to address high class imbalance. The optimized MLP model demonstrated superior performance, achieving a 0.9 accuracy and 0.88 F1-score on the test set, proving the efficacy of deep learning in identifying complex fraud patterns with limited data.
